## Building a Top of Mind CRM System

To stay ahead, you need a structured CRM workflow. Here's a blueprint to keep you in sync with clients:

### Step 1: Initial Inquiry Trigger

**Trigger**: A prospective client fills out a contact form.

**Action**: Log the lead into your CRM and send an automated welcome email offering to schedule a call or provide initial service information.

**Outcome**: Immediate engagement builds trust and captures attention while the lead is warm.

### Step 2: Follow-Up Sequence

**Trigger**: No response within 24 hours.

**Action**: Send a personalized follow-up email, offering a free consultation or home loan guide.

**Outcome**: Keeps your services top of mind and demonstrates persistence, increasing conversion likelihood.

### Step 3: Active Lead Engagement

**Trigger**: Client schedules a call or meeting.

**Action**: Send a calendar invite with a personalized message and a reminder 24 hours before the appointment.

**Outcome**: Reduces no-show rates and reinforces professionalism.

## Before and After: The Transformation

Before a top of mind CRM: scattered notes, missed follow-ups, unpredictable schedules. After: streamlined workflows with automated reminders prompt timely interactions, freeing you to focus on personalized client care. The transformation boosts efficiency, client satisfaction, and loyalty.
