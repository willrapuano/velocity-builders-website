## The System: Building a Workflow Blueprint

A robust CRM strategy goes beyond choosing a platform. It's about crafting a workflow that aligns with your business goals. Here's a blueprint to get started:

1. **Lead Capture and Entry**: Automatically capture leads from your website or landing pages directly into your CRM. A form submission triggers the creation of a new contact profile.

2. **Automated Follow-Up**: Set triggers for immediate follow-up emails or texts once a lead enters your system. This ensures no time is wasted, and your potential client feels valued from the start.

3. **Pipeline Management**: Use your CRM to track deal status. Set automated reminders for follow-up actions as leads progress through your sales pipeline.

4. **Client Communication**: Automate regular updates to clients about their application status. Trigger these communications based on key milestones in the loan process.

5. **Post-Closing Engagement**: Maintain relationships post-closing. Automate follow-ups to request referrals or offer additional services.
