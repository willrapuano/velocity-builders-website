## The System: Integrating Your CRM with Your Website

To succeed, you need robust CRM integration. Follow this step-by-step guide:

1. **Lead Capture Forms**: Embed lead capture forms on your website. Connect them to your CRM so every lead is automatically logged.
   
2. **Automated Responses**: Set up triggers for immediate automated emails or texts acknowledging inquiries. This should occur within seconds.

3. **Lead Scoring**: Implement lead scoring in your CRM to prioritize leads based on engagement and readiness to buy.

4. **Task Automation**: Automate follow-up reminders and tasks. If a lead doesn't respond within 48 hours, your CRM should prompt a call.

5. **Analytics and Reporting**: Ensure your CRM tracks key metrics like conversion rates and lead sources. Use this data to refine your approach.
