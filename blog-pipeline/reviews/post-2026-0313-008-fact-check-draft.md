1. **Lead Capture Automation**: Use website and partner site forms to auto-populate leads into your CRM.
2. **Immediate Response Triggers**: Set triggers for instant text or email responses to new inquiries, ensuring no lead feels neglected.
3. **Follow-up Sequences**: Automate follow-up sequences that personalize outreach based on client behavior and preferences.
4. **Pipeline Management**: Create a visual sales pipeline that automates status updates and reminds you of critical deadlines.
5. **Client Nurturing**: Implement automated drip campaigns to maintain engagement with leads not yet ready to commit.
