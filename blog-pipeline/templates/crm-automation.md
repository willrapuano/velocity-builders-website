## Tone & Voice
- Structure: Thought leadership — teach frameworks, show data, explain systems
- Voice: Aggressive and direct — no soft language, no hedging
- Formula: "Here's what's broken. Here's how to fix it. Here's what it costs you to wait."
- Think: Teach like a professor, write like a closer

## Title Pattern

## Template Variables
- **concept**: {{concept}}
- **audience**: {{audience}}
- **year**: {{year}}

## Structure Requirements
1. **Hook**: Open with a CRM horror story or automation failure stat.
2. **The Cost of Manual**: Quantify what bad/no CRM costs in time, money, deals lost.
3. **The System**: Step-by-step workflow or automation blueprint. Be specific about triggers, actions, timing.
4. **Before/After**: Show the transformation — manual process vs automated.
5. **ROI Math**: Simple calculation showing the return on setting this up.
6. **Implementation Priority**: What to set up first, second, third.

## Rules
- Word count: 1,200-1,800 words
- NEVER mention GoHighLevel, Follow Up Boss, KvCORE, or any specific CRM by name
- Use "your CRM", "automation platform", "your system" instead
- No Pruitt Title CTAs or endorsements
- Include specific workflow examples (trigger → action → outcome)
- Year tag {{year}} in title and body
- Heading hierarchy (STRICT): ## (H2) for main sections (4–6 per post), ### (H3) sparingly — max 2–3 total, never back-to-back, never a list of H3s; NO #### or deeper; NO H1 in body (page renders the title); DO NOT repeat the title as a heading
