## The System: Automating Your Follow-Up

To combat these inefficiencies, you need a strategic system in place. Here's how to build an effective follow-up email workflow using your CRM:

1. **Trigger**: Set the trigger for your follow-up email as soon as a new lead enters the system. This can be through web inquiries, open house sign-ups, or direct calls.
   
2. **Action**: The CRM automatically sends a personalized welcome email within 24 hours. This email should introduce you, your services, and what the lead can expect next.

3. **Timing**: Schedule the next follow-up email for one week later. This email should provide valuable insights or listings that match the lead’s interests, keeping your services top of mind.

4. **Ongoing Engagement**: Continue the sequence with bi-weekly emails that offer market updates, success stories, or tips for home buyers/sellers.

5. **Outcome**: Your CRM tracks engagement metrics (opens, clicks, responses) to prioritize leads who require immediate attention.

By automating this sequence, you ensure no lead goes unnoticed, and your communication remains consistent.
