## The System: Automating Inbound Marketing Stages

To truly master inbound marketing stages, you must implement a system that automates and optimizes every phase. Here’s a step-by-step workflow to transform your approach:

### Attract Stage

**Trigger:** A potential client visits your website or social media page.

**Action:** Automatically capture their information through a form or chatbot.

**Outcome:** Their details are entered directly into your CRM, triggering a personalized introductory email that outlines your services.

### Engage Stage

**Trigger:** The client opens the introductory email or clicks on a link.

**Action:** Launch a series of automated follow-up emails offering valuable content, such as builder case studies or home design tips.

**Outcome:** The client is nurtured through informative content, building trust and increasing the likelihood of a sale.

### Delight Stage

**Trigger:** The client completes a purchase or signs a contract.

**Action:** Send automated thank-you messages and request feedback.

**Outcome:** Enhances client satisfaction and generates testimonials for future marketing efforts.

## Before/After: The Transformation from Manual to Automated

Consider the builder who manually tracked every lead and sale, spending hours each week on repetitive tasks. Before automation, their follow-up rate was dismal, and client satisfaction suffered due to inconsistent communication.

## Implementation Priority: What to Set Up First

1. **Lead Capture Automation:** Start by automating the process of capturing leads from your website and social platforms. This ensures no potential client is lost in the shuffle.

2. **Email Nurture Sequences:** Develop automated email campaigns tailored to different stages of the client journey. Focus on delivering value and building trust.

3. **Feedback and Referral Requests:** After a successful sale, automate the process of gathering client feedback and requesting referrals to create a cycle of continuous business growth.
