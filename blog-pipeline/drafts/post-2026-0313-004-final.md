## Step-by-Step Framework to Asking for Referrals

Transform your referral strategy with a structured approach. Here's how to fix passive referral gathering and create an aggressive lead-generation machine.

### Step 1: Identify Your Top Referrers

- **Analyze Your Client Base**: Use your CRM to segment clients based on satisfaction and engagement. Identify past referrers.
- **Create a List**: Focus on the top 20% of clients who can provide 80% of your referrals. Prioritize growth potential.

### Step 2: Craft Your Referral Request

- **Personalize the Message**: Use the client's name, refer to specific projects, and express genuine appreciation.
- **Be Direct and Specific**: Clearly state you're seeking referrals and the type of clients you want.

### Step 3: Choose the Right Timing

- **Post-Completion Touchpoint**: Ask for referrals immediately after project completion when satisfaction peaks.
- **Follow-Up**: Schedule automated reminders in your CRM to follow up a month later. Consistency is key.

### Step 4: Offer an Incentive

- **Tangible Rewards**: Implement a referral program with rewards like discounts or gift cards.
- **Exclusive Offers**: Make offers time-sensitive to create urgency.

### Step 5: Show Appreciation

- **Thank You Notes**: Send personalized thank-you notes to all referrers. Gratitude goes a long way.
- **Public Recognition**: Recognize top referrers in newsletters or on social media, with permission.
