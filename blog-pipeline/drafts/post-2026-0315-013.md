## The System: Integrating Your CRM with Your Website

To turn the tide, you need a robust CRM integration with your website. Here’s a step-by-step guide to building a seamless system:

1. **Lead Capture Forms**: Start by embedding lead capture forms directly into your website. Ensure these forms are connected to your CRM so that every lead is automatically logged.
   
2. **Automated Responses**: Set up triggers so that when a lead submits a form, your CRM immediately sends an automated email or text acknowledging their inquiry. This should happen within seconds, not minutes.

3. **Lead Scoring**: Implement lead scoring within your CRM. This system prioritizes leads based on their engagement level and readiness to buy, ensuring you focus on the most promising prospects.

4. **Task Automation**: Use your CRM to automate follow-up reminders and tasks. For instance, if a lead hasn't responded to your initial email within 48 hours, your CRM should prompt you to call them.

5. **Analytics and Reporting**: Finally, ensure your CRM can track and report on key metrics like conversion rates, lead sources, and the effectiveness of your follow-up sequences. This data is invaluable for fine-tuning your approach.

### The Role of Timing

In real estate, timing is everything. With a properly integrated CRM, the time from lead capture to first contact can be reduced to under five minutes. This responsiveness is a game-changer, as it can increase conversion rates by up to 400%.
