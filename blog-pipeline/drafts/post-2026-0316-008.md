## Understanding SEO Header Tags

Before diving into optimization, it's crucial to understand what SEO header tags are. These are HTML elements (H1, H2, H3, etc.) that help structure your content, making it easier for search engines to index and rank your pages. They signal the importance of the content within your page and guide both users and search engines through your site. As a builder, your website must clearly communicate its value proposition, and header tags are the roadmap.

## Implementing a Workflow for SEO Header Tags

Here's a step-by-step workflow to optimize your SEO header tags effectively:

1. **Content Audit**: Begin by auditing your existing website content. Identify which pages lack structure and prioritize them for header tag optimization.
   
2. **Keyword Research**: Determine the primary and secondary keywords for each page. Ensure these keywords are naturally incorporated into your header tags.

3. **H1 Tag Optimization**: Your H1 tag should include the primary keyword and clearly define the page's main topic. Each page should only have one H1 tag.

4. **Hierarchy of Tags**: Use H2 tags for main sections and H3 tags for subsections. Maintain a logical flow to guide both users and search engines through your content.

5. **Content Consistency**: Ensure that the content under each header tag is relevant to the tag itself. Misleading headers can harm your search rankings.

6. **Review and Revise**: Regularly review your header tags as part of your SEO strategy to adapt to changes in search algorithms and market trends.
